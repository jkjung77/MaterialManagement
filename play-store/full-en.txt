A factory material app for the shop floor. Record inbound stock, production, and inventory on the phone even when the network is weak. When connected, the same factory stays in sync across phones.

Materials
• Item number, name, unit, price, safety stock, lead time
• Inbound, outbound, scrap, and stocktake adjustments
• Current stock = opening + inbound − production use − outbound − scrap + adjust
• Find by name, number, or barcode

Single-part production
• Bill of materials with usage per part
• Daily production automatically consumes materials
• The month plan is entered on the production screen. A finished-goods plan is only a reference

Barcode
• Auto reads the code in the center guide
• Manual reads the code under the crosshair when you tap, for sheets of many codes

Finished goods
• Built from single parts
• Monthly plan and daily finished output
• Finished output consumes component parts. Materials are consumed by single-part production

Results and close
• Sales, material usage, purchases, and the material ratio
• Pick a date to see the single parts and finished goods made that day
• Month close locks the month and carries stock forward
• Stocktake differences need manager approval

Access
• Enter with the factory password
• Editing materials, parts, and finished goods needs the manager password, or a grant from the manager
• Month close and stocktake approval stay with the manager

Backup and sync
• Works offline on the phone
• A linked factory syncs with the server
• Export a JSON backup anytime

A banner ad may appear at the bottom. Barcode scanning uses the camera. No location or contacts permission.

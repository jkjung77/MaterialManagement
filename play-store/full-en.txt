A factory material app for the shop floor. Record inbound stock, daily production, and inventory on the phone even when the network is weak.

Materials
• Item number, name, unit, unit price, safety stock, lead time
• Inbound, outbound, scrap, and stocktake adjustments
• Current stock = opening + inbound − production use − outbound − scrap + adjust

Single-part production
• Bill of materials with US per part
• Daily production automatically consumes materials
• Material cost, material ratio, plan versus actual

Finished goods
• Built from single parts
• Monthly customer plan only (no daily sales entry)

Close and backup
• Month close locks results and carries stock to next month
• Manager approval for stocktake differences
• All data stays on the device. Export a JSON backup anytime

No ads. No sign-in. No location or contacts permission.
